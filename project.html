FAQs for Cakes



Will the cake be fresh?


Yes. Each cake is freshly baked by expert bakers with high-quality and premium ingredients.


Can I customize the cake with a message or design?


Absolutely! You can customize the cake with different sizes, flavors, colors, designs and themes.


What should I do if I have issues with the cake after it arrives?


If the cake is damaged or spoiled, you can contact us within 48 hours of delivery for a replacement.


Can I order a cake for a specific time or date?


Yes. You can select the date and time slot that fits your schedule.


How do I choose the right size of cake for a party?


For a small gathering of 3-4 people, a 500 gm cake is ideal. Larger parties may require bigger cakes.


Are there options for specific dietary needs?


Yes, we offer options such as sugar-free, eggless and gluten-free cakes.


What is the shelf life of the cakes delivered?


Our cakes can stay fresh for 2-3 days when stored properly.
